# Editorial illustrations

Original images generated with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool for this project. Decorative imagery does not represent a real family, residence, service or recommended course of action. WebP files are served from this repository, with no external image requests.

- `pathways.webp`: Tactile gouache and coloured-pencil Australian eucalyptus landscape with a winding footpath, coastal hills, small home and two birds. Sage, olive, cream and muted ochre palette; quiet daylight; no people, writing, logos or dramatic imagery.
- `your-notes.webp`: Matching editorial still life with a blank notebook, forest-green pencil, sage cup and eucalyptus in an amber vase on a sunlit wooden desk. Warm ivory and muted green palette; no writing, people or logos.

The generation prompts are recorded here in condensed form. Both assets were generated using the built-in tool, inspected, and encoded as WebP for web delivery.

## Complete generation prompts

### Pathways

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: editorial hero illustration for Beyond Stay or Leave, a calm NSW child-focused public-information classroom website with forest green and warm ivory branding. Create a refined tactile gouache and colored-pencil landscape, wide 3:2 composition: a gently winding footpath through native Australian eucalyptus trees and soft grassy hills towards a sunlit coastal horizon, a small welcoming home partly visible amid foliage, two subtle birds. Restrained sage green, olive, pale butter yellow, warm cream, muted terracotta accents, delicate paper texture, sophisticated magazine illustration, organic contours and soft light. Quiet, welcoming, hopeful, without implying any particular choice is safe. No people, no dramatic imagery, no text, no letters, no logos, no border, no watermark. Rich enough to be a beautiful website focal point, uncluttered composition.

### Your notes

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: small editorial illustration for the My Case Summary section of a child-focused NSW information website. Create a sophisticated tactile gouache and colored-pencil still life in a wide 3:2 composition: an open blank cream notebook on a warm wooden desk, a forest-green pencil, a sage ceramic cup, a small eucalyptus stem in an amber glass vase, soft daylight from an unseen window. Warm ivory paper texture, muted sage and deep forest greens, ochre and gentle terracotta details. Beautiful organic shapes and detailed hand-painted surfaces, calm editorial magazine quality, welcoming and understated, lots of breathing room. No writing on the notebook or anywhere else, no text, no letters, no people, no logos, no watermark, no borders. Suitable as a wide crop beside existing webpage text.
