# Editorial illustrations

## Current supportive illustrations

The homepage now uses `support-together.webp`: a mother being listened to by a supportive woman while her children play nearby. The summary entry uses `supported-planning.webp`: a mother holding the pen and reviewing information beside a supportive woman. Both are fictional editorial illustrations generated with the built-in image generation tool, then encoded as WebP. They depict companionship and agency, without presenting a real client or service. Earlier landscape and notebook assets remain in the repository as previous versions.

### Support together — complete prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: supportive editorial hero illustration for an English-language NSW information website for mothers experiencing domestic and family violence. Depict a mother with two children, a school-age daughter and a younger son, seated comfortably with a supportive adult woman in a welcoming community support room. The two adult women sit at equal eye level, the supporter listens attentively with an open, respectful posture, the mother is an active participant with dignity and choice; children nearby drawing or playing calmly. Show human connection, being heard, practical support and companionship, with natural gently hopeful expressions rather than exaggerated smiles. Sophisticated contemporary hand-painted gouache and coloured-pencil editorial illustration with soft shapes, warm cream paper, sage and forest green, muted terracotta and ochre. Wide 3:2 composition, people grouped centrally, warm daylight and a little foliage. No bruises, no distressed victim stereotype, no threatening figure, no rescue symbolism, no promise of guaranteed safety, no text, no letters, no logos, no watermark. Fictional illustrated people, not a representation of a real service or client.

### Supported planning — complete prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: warm supportive editorial illustration beside the My Case Summary entry of a website for mothers experiencing domestic and family violence. Show two adult women, a mother and a supportive community worker or peer, seated side by side at a table reviewing a blank notebook together. The mother holds the pen and makes her own decisions; the other woman listens with an open respectful posture and does not take over. Equal partnership, being heard, practical support, agency and dignity. Focus on their upper bodies and shared workspace; calm natural expressions, gentle eye contact. Refined contemporary hand-painted gouache and coloured-pencil illustration, warm cream paper texture, sage and forest green clothing, small muted terracotta and ochre accents, soft daylight and an unobtrusive plant. Wide 3:2 composition suitable for a landscape crop. No writing or text in the notebook or anywhere, no visible personal records, no logos, no watermark, no bruises, no crying stereotype, no rescue or saviour symbolism, no exaggerated happiness. Fictional illustration, not a real client or service.

Original images generated with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool for this project. Decorative imagery does not represent a real family, residence, service or recommended course of action. WebP files are served from this repository, with no external image requests.

- `pathways.webp`: Tactile gouache and coloured-pencil Australian eucalyptus landscape with a winding footpath, coastal hills, small home and two birds. Sage, olive, cream and muted ochre palette; quiet daylight; no people, writing, logos or dramatic imagery.
- `your-notes.webp`: Matching editorial still life with a blank notebook, forest-green pencil, sage cup and eucalyptus in an amber vase on a sunlit wooden desk. Warm ivory and muted green palette; no writing, people or logos.

The generation prompts are recorded here in condensed form. Both assets were generated using the built-in tool, inspected, and encoded as WebP for web delivery.

## Complete generation prompts

### Pathways

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: editorial hero illustration for Beyond Stay or Leave, a calm NSW child-focused public-information classroom website with forest green and warm ivory branding. Create a refined tactile gouache and colored-pencil landscape, wide 3:2 composition: a gently winding footpath through native Australian eucalyptus trees and soft grassy hills towards a sunlit coastal horizon, a small welcoming home partly visible amid foliage, two subtle birds. Restrained sage green, olive, pale butter yellow, warm cream, muted terracotta accents, delicate paper texture, sophisticated magazine illustration, organic contours and soft light. Quiet, welcoming, hopeful, without implying any particular choice is safe. No people, no dramatic imagery, no text, no letters, no logos, no border, no watermark. Rich enough to be a beautiful website focal point, uncluttered composition.

### Your notes

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: small editorial illustration for the My Case Summary section of a child-focused NSW information website. Create a sophisticated tactile gouache and colored-pencil still life in a wide 3:2 composition: an open blank cream notebook on a warm wooden desk, a forest-green pencil, a sage ceramic cup, a small eucalyptus stem in an amber glass vase, soft daylight from an unseen window. Warm ivory paper texture, muted sage and deep forest greens, ochre and gentle terracotta details. Beautiful organic shapes and detailed hand-painted surfaces, calm editorial magazine quality, welcoming and understated, lots of breathing room. No writing on the notebook or anywhere else, no text, no letters, no people, no logos, no watermark, no borders. Suitable as a wide crop beside existing webpage text.
